AN AMBITIOUS VISION FOR A CARDLESS FUTURE, A CLASS STRATEGY OF GOING FORWARD, STRONG BUSINESS MOMENTUM & AN INSPIRED ORGANIZATION

AYN is an amalgam of future-ready technologies focused on creating products for the next generation. AYN is not just a company, it’s a vision of a greater future for all of us. For more than a decade AYN InfoTech has believed in building a responsible business operation which means not just to be legally compliant but also to be empathetic to all the stakeholders.

Our business ethics and operations are guided by our vision to be the most customer-centric and valued Information Technology brand in the Industry. Our vision is at the very heart of everything we do.

Since 2009 we have been formalising sustainability strategies for our various business operations. We are adapting confidently to the changing business environment and operational requirements through our robust change management processes, backed up by our value principles.

We are determined to optimally utilise our talent pool since they are the most important assets of our company. With our engagement and nurturing strategies for talent along with encouragement and support, we together can make a great contribution for every stakeholder of the company - our clients, business partners, vendors and community.

We believe in talent development through encouragement, support and training so that our employees are empowered to conceptualise, develop and implement innovative, futuristic and brilliant solutions for our clients and partners.

We have seen a lot of progress in terms of building a stakeholder friendly and sustainable business with our focus on internal and external CSR activities and we will continue our efforts in the same direction.
