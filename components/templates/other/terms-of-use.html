Privacy Policy

When you use our services, you are putting trust in us by sharing your information. We understand this responsibility and protect your data effectively.

Basis

The basis of data privacy protection policy is to provide comprehensive information about the collection, processing, and use of your data.

This privacy policy is meant for you to understand:

  • How we use your information
  • How you can update your data
  • How you can export or delete your data
  • What rights you have concerning our data processing

This data privacy policy is applicable only for the use of personal data on our website ayninfotech.com, including all the subpages. This policy is inapplicable to any information collected from an offline source, social media or via channels other than this website.

How we use your information

We use your information in various ways, including:

  • Operate, and maintain our website.
  • Personalize, improve, and expand our website.
  • Analyze and understand how you use our website.
  • Develop new products, services, features, and functionalities.
  • Communicate directly or through our partners, customer service, and provide you with updates regarding the website and marketing purposes.
  • Send you emails and updates.
  • Analyze, find, and prevent fraud.

Log Files

AYN InfoTech Limited follows a standard method of using log files. When you drop by our website, your browser sends data to our servers. We use this information to maintain and establish connections. This private data also include your IP address. Moreover, the following data is also collected:

  • Browser used.
  • Operating system information.
  • Source from which you reached the website.
  • Connecting site (next site).
  • Referrer (previous site).
  • IP address.
  • Internet service provider.
  • Number of clicks.

The purpose of the information is to analyze trends, administer the site, track users' movement on the website, and gather demographic information.

Contact Form

You can send us a query at any time through the contact form on our website; you will be asked to enter full name, mobile number and email address.

The above data is mandatory. Any other information provided by you is provided voluntarily. The information provided by you will be used for further communication by our team.

The legal basis depends on your query. Usually, our legitimate interest in making available the contact functionality and replying to your question. If your query relates to a conclusion of any contract with us, then processing will be based on this pre-contractual responsibility.

Your data will be deleted when your query has been resolved. Where we enter into a contract, if necessary, we will continue to process the data for the contract.

Third-Party Privacy Policies

AYN Infotech Limited's Privacy Policy does not refer to other websites or advertisements. Thus, we advise you to discuss the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information. It may include their instructions and practices about how to opt-out of certain options. You can also select to disable cookies from the browser you used.
